Frequently asked questions

Is ClearCut really free?

Yes. ClearCut is a free background remover with no signup, no watermark, and no per-image fee. Remove as many backgrounds as you want.

Does it reduce image quality?

No. You keep full resolution. PNG export is lossless and transparent. JPEG and WEBP include a quality slider only if you want a smaller file.

Are my photos uploaded to any server?

No. Processing runs entirely in your browser. Photos are not uploaded to ClearCut or sent to a third-party API. See the privacy page.

What image formats are supported?

Upload JPG, PNG, or WEBP. Download as PNG (transparent), JPEG, or WEBP.

Can I use it commercially?

Yes. Photos you export are yours to use in commercial work. The AGPL-3.0 license covers the ClearCut software, not your downloaded images.
